# About DreamKnowSelf

Welcome! I'm SG, and this is my space for exploring the big questions—philosophy, mysticism, astrology, psychology, meditation, and spirituality—with humor, honesty, and a healthy dose of skepticism.

## Why This Blog?

Because all these topics inevitably link to self-knowledge and awareness of dreams and reality. Hence the name: **DreamKnowSelf**.

## My Approach

I try to write with:
- **Humor** - Actually attempt on it, Because serious topics don't have to be serious all the time
- **Honesty** - I usually question things, including my own beliefs.
- **Gentle interrogation** - Asking "but wait, does this actually work?"
- **Observation** - Noticing patterns, contradictions, and the absurd

## What You'll Find Here

- **Philosophy** - Deep dives into eternal questions (with jokes)
- **Astrology** - Honest takes on what i learnt, not fortune-telling.
- **Book Reviews** - Funny, honest reviews of books that made me think
- **Spirituality** - Exploring practices, ideas, and the spaces between them


*"The unexamined life is not worth living." — Socrates*
